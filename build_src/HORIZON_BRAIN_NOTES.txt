TRADER ROBOT 71.7.27 — MULTI-HORIZON ENDPOINT BRAIN

What changed:
1. Removed the unsupported Logical Risk experiment by starting from the pre-Logical-Risk trajectory source.
2. Kept the existing audited Direction/Entry gates unchanged.
3. Every observation now exports the full UP-vs-DOWN score surface for 60/120/180/240/300 seconds.
4. Expiry is selected from the accumulated endpoint evidence across the whole minute, not from the mode of previously chosen expiry labels.
5. Keeps ~29 observations/cycle, temporal trajectory diagnostics, compact visual memory, manual EXECUTED and WIN/LOSS/VOID.
6. CONF remains CALIBRATING. Scores are diagnostic, not probabilities.
7. No automatic trade execution.

Evidence motivating the change:
- 040420 sample: 11 WIN / 7 LOSS / 1 VOID; UP60 9W/4L.
- 053220 Logical-Risk sample: 8 WIN / 13 LOSS; UP60 3W/7L.
- Logical Risk did not generalize (2W/2L among logical-risk-pass trades).
- Entry quality, margin, and consistency alone did not separate wins from losses reliably across both samples.
- Therefore this build does not invent a new quality threshold. It changes the representation of the expiry problem:
  each horizon is evaluated directly and temporally.

Research basis:
- Multi-horizon forecasting should preserve horizon-specific information rather than reuse one generic directional label.
- Financial markets exhibit concept drift; fixed relationships can fail across regimes.
- Backtest selection must be evaluated out-of-sample to avoid data-snooping/overfitting.

This is an experimental signal-only build. Test on Demo and export memory before judging it.
